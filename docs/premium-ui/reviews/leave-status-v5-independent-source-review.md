## Semantics finding and resolution

Backend source was read only. `leave.py` defines active calendar statuses as approved/requested/imported pending review and blocking status as approved; `leave_day_entries` emits each active record per intersecting day without deduplication. `leave_summary` counts active records and unique active people separately. Cancelled/rejected records therefore belong in the complete request cards/table/history, not fabricated active-calendar fixtures.

The v2/v3 guidance that requested/imported leave are simply “warnings until approved” was too broad. `rota_safety.person_blockers` does separate approved hard blockers from pending warnings, but `balance_duties.py:632` structural eligibility excludes all active overlapping leave; `rota_template.pressure_for_slot` also includes active overlapping records in staffing unavailability. No backend normalization was authorized here. The v4/v5 replacement correctly scopes the presentation: “Status counts include approved records and requests awaiting review. Pending requests are not confirmed absence. Review returned blocking counts and duty safety checks before assigning; eligibility depends on the operation.” It makes no promise that pending records can always receive duties.
