WHY THIRD-CALL NIGHT COUNTS DIFFER
The 58 third-call doctors fall into seven groups with different permitted duty
types. Within each group, the night gap is 0-2 and the total-duty gap is 0-1.
The larger overall night gap crosses those groups. Tested replacement combinations
were blocked by existing call-subgroup or unit rules, which remain enforced.
This comparison is descriptive: matching duty types does not mean identical dated
availability, rest or staffing. It does not prove that no better rota is possible,
and it does not replace the primary call-level fairness target.
See November2026_third_call_fairness_explanation.xlsx for the anonymous groups/chart.

WHAT THE ENGINE NOW DOES
- Admin can choose equal counts or availability-based fairness. Half-days count
  as 0.5, overlapping leave is counted once, and proportional targets use the
  duties actually covered safely. Planned demand remains visible separately.
- Ordinary AND extra duties require a dated posting to an included monthly unit.
  The engine does not invent missing postings or create an extra-only category.
- Effective call levels, explicitly permitted pools, leave, rest, overlap,
  capacity, unit rules, individual limits and protected assignments remain checks.
- The new night refinement exchanges ordinary duties while preserving each
  person's total duties, weekends and extra credits. Hours inequality cannot
  increase. It shares the existing work budget and shows its search bounds.
- Repeated draft changes become one original-to-final change per assignment.
  Real three-row Apply/Verify/Undo tests restore the original holders exactly.

BUGS FIXED DURING THIS AUDIT
Short-duty leave overlaps, an existing unattributed weekend-limit excess,
unsafe Undo restoration of quota breaches, and Undo input locking when OT was
switched off were corrected. Reports no longer bootstrap rule data while reading.
Preview/checker/report work was reduced without changing their timing thresholds.
The review screen now shows final hours after night refinement, rather than an
intermediate result from the earlier hours pass. Failed historical tests remain
in the execution records; they have not been counted as passes.
