## Accounting contract for N20/N22/N23

1. Read canonical LeaveRequest rows through existing workspace/report services, not the 30-row recent-profile subset. Query overlap against explicit inclusive report dates; clip only for reporting, preserving original ranges/IDs. Return the report range, explicit as-of local facility date/time, calculation version and coverage limitations. Do not infer a complete historical ledger from the oldest record or absence of rows.
2. Show request-record counts by canonical status/type separately from person-time measures. Approved, pending, rejected, cancelled and unrecognized status remain distinct. Unknown values must be disclosed; never count them as approved. Pending means requested or imported_pending_review; stress capacity is a separate scenario.
3. Call the elapsed measure **recorded approved absence elapsed**, not verified attendance/actual leave taken. Dates and approval do not prove someone actually stayed away. As-of clipping separates elapsed, currently in progress and future approved intervals. The interface may describe this as a leave-taken estimate only with that qualification. Lifecycle history is required to reconstruct what was known at an earlier as-of date; current rows alone give current-state estimates.
4. Represent intervals as half-open local wall-clock bands: FULL_DAY [00:00,next 00:00), AM [00:00,12:00), PM [12:00,next 00:00), NIGHT [00:00,08:00) and [18:00,next 00:00) on EACH stored inclusive date. NIGHT is not automatically anchored to the following date. Exact end/start contact is no overlap. Use existing scheduling clock conventions; timezone/DST elapsed hours are not inferred from naive stored dates.
5. For each person/status category, union overlapping approved intervals before counting covered hours or distinct covered calendar dates. Never add fractions from duplicate records. FULL_DAY plus AM on one date remains 24 hours and one calendar date; AM plus NIGHT covers 18 hours; AM+PM+NIGHT covers 24. Record counts remain separate. CUSTOM/unknown bands must expose unresolved accounting; conservative safety fallback may be shown separately, never silently charged as exact leave taken.
6. Publish covered local clock hours and distinct covered calendar dates, with an explicit counting basis. Do not label 12 hours as an entitlement half-day, 14 NIGHT hours as a policy fraction, or touched dates as full days taken. Do not silently divide hours by 24 to produce leave deductions. Today's partially elapsed date is explicitly in progress; retain that distinction alongside elapsed hours.
7. Type breakdown: retain source record counts; time attributed to multiple types is overlap/shared, not independently additive. Overall approved union is authoritative for total time. Preserve both sources; no arbitrary winner. Pending overlapping approval is not additional approved time. Rejection/cancellation contributes zero current approved time but remains visible in lifecycle/request statistics.
8. Calendar-date reporting includes weekends/holidays in its explicitly labeled basis. Working-day totals require an authoritative dated individual work calendar; without one return unavailable and explain. A weekday count is not a clinical working-day count. Dated unit/call attribution must follow actual postings/transfers; no entire-range first-date attribution.
9. No entitlement, remaining balance, accrual, carry-forward, sanction or disciplinary rank is inferred. Leave type ON_DUTY/CONFERENCE/OFF does not on its own define chargeable personal leave; retain separate labeled type evidence. No score penalizes approved leave.
10. History must link existing audit, member activity, batch/source and bin records with actor/time and source reference where available. Distinguish recorded source text from verified provenance, missing legacy events from reconstructed facts, and deletion from cancellation. Do not invent actors or backfill historical approvals as newly observed events. Existing source IDs remain the source of truth.

## Fixed worked examples

All examples are one person, November 2026 unless specified. As-of is **14 November 2026 12:00 local**; reporting window [1 November 00:00,1 December 00:00). Each row is independent unless combined explicitly. Hours are covered local clock hours, not entitlement deductions.

| Canonical inputs | Expected reporting outcome |
|---|---|
| Approved FULL_DAY 10–12 November | 1 approved request; 3 elapsed covered calendar dates; 72 elapsed hours; 0 future hours. Recorded approval is not attendance confirmation. |
| Approved FULL_DAY 20–21 November | 1 approved request; 0 elapsed hours; 48 future hours on 2 dates. |
| Approved FULL_DAY 14 November | 12 elapsed hours, 12 future hours; 1 in-progress date; not one completed day. |
| Requested FULL_DAY 10–12 November | 1 pending request, 0 approved elapsed hours; potential interval 72 hours shown only as pending/stress. |
| Imported_pending_review FULL_DAY 20 November | 1 import-pending request; 0 approved hours; separate 24-hour future pending interval. |
| Cancelled or rejected FULL_DAY 10–12 November | 1 respective status record, 0 current approved hours; previous approval only when supported by events. |
| Approved AM 10 November plus another approved AM same date | 2 approved records; union 12 elapsed hours, 1 touched date; no double deduction. |
| Approved AM plus PM 10 November | 2 records, 24 union hours on 1 date; individual records remain traceable. |
| Approved AM plus NIGHT 10 November | 2 records; union [00:00,12:00) and [18:00,24:00), 18 hours. NIGHT overlaps AM during 00:00–08:00. |
| Approved FULL_DAY plus AM 10 November, different types | 2 records, 24 total union hours; AM's 12 hours are shared across type attribution. Type-hours cannot be summed as the total. |
| Approved FULL_DAY 31 October–2 November | Original request stays 3 inclusive dates; November view clips to 2 dates/48 elapsed hours; October clips to 1 date. |
| Approved NIGHT 30 November | [30 Nov 00:00,08:00) plus [30 Nov 18:00,1 Dec 00:00); 14 future hours within November, none in December. |
| Approved FULL_DAY 28 February–1 March 2028 | 3 original dates; leap-year February clips to 28/29 February, 48 hours; March to 24 hours. |
| Approved CUSTOM or unexpected slot, 10 November | Unknown precise accounting; conservative full-date safety fallback explicitly labeled, never 24 verified leave hours. |
| No matching records in a requested year | Zero records found within known query scope; historical completeness and working-day/entitlement balances remain unavailable. |
